\title[Operationalizing Screening Off]{Operationalizing Screening Off for Rainforest Admission}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Patrick} \sur{Glenn}}\email{pwmglenn@outlook.com}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{Independent Researcher}, \orgaddress{\city{East Providence}, \country{USA}}}

\abstract{Scale-relative structural realism allows that real patterns may occupy more than one level of ontology, but the admittance criteria for those levels remain underdetermined. Franklin and Robertson (2024) offer the most developed recent proposal: emergence, understood as screening off plus novelty, earns an entity its place in the rainforest. This paper argues that their criterion is the right one and that its stated test underdetermines it. Conditional independence admits an observational reading, on which micro-detail is redundant given the macrostate across the system's actual trajectory, and a modal reading, on which that redundancy survives admissible intervention. The readings coincide in the central cases and diverge where apparent macro-autonomy is borrowed from off-partition structure. A macro-variable driven by a shared upstream cause can render micro-details conditionally irrelevant and support novel macrodependencies under passive observation, while distinct micro-realizations of its macrostate come apart under direct perturbation. A structurally different hidden-feedback case shows the same signature. On the modal reading, screening off and transition autonomy are not two conditions but one: to screen off micro-detail from the macro-future under admissible perturbation is to carry autonomous transition structure at that grain. Closure is the admissibility-indexed test that recovers this modal content. A candidate qualifies when, for a fixed regime, horizon, and admissible intervention class, macrostate information is sufficient for macro-transitions, so within-class micro-differences do not change macro-level what-follows. In exact Markov settings, strong lumpability provides a benchmark realization. In non-ideal settings, closure is graded through leakiness and convergent diagnostics under fixed constraints, with explicit downgrade rules. The interventionist machinery is compatible with structural realism because interventions are used as diagnostic probes for relational stability, not as metaphysical primitives. The contribution is therefore a correction to how an existing filter is read and applied rather than a rival to it: Dennett's compression identifies candidate patterns, screening off and novelty supply the filter, and closure specifies the reading of that filter under which admission is earned.}

\keywords{real patterns, rainforest realism, closure, strong lumpability, coarse-graining, structural realism, interventionism}
