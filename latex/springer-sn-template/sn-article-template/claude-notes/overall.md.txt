### Working rule for future revisions

Any new argumentative use of these sources should satisfy three conditions:

1. We have the relevant text locally, or we clearly mark the claim as bibliographic rather than textual.

3. We do not let the revised paper sound more certain about another author's commitments than the source itself warrants.

## 1. THE CORE REPOSITIONING

**From:** "Dennett's compression is too permissive → closure fixes it"

**To:** "Rainforest Realism lacks a formal admittance criterion → closure provides it, building on and going beyond Franklin & Robertson's screening-off condition"

**Narrative arc:** Dennett gave us real patterns → Ladyman & Ross gave us the rainforest → Franklin & Robertson (2024) gave us the best current admittance criterion (screening-off + novelty) → screening-off is necessary but insufficient because parasitic macro-variables satisfy it → closure fills the remaining gap.

**Key principle:** The paper is *completing* the rainforest, not competing with it. Position closure as independently motivated from dynamical considerations that *happens to* fill a gap, rather than designed to serve it.

## 3. THREE PHILOSOPHICAL TENSIONS TO ADDRESS

### 3a. The Causation Problem
Ladyman & Ross are broadly Russellian about causation (skeptical it's fundamental). Your framework is thoroughly Woodwardian/interventionist. **Resolution:** Intervention isn't metaphysically fundamental in the paper — it's a *diagnostic probe* for structural autonomy. The structure is what's real (consistent with OSR). The intervention tests whether it's genuinely autonomous. Cite Ladyman & Lorenzetti (2023) on effective OSR as compatible with this move. **Needs its own subsection.**

### 3b. The Primacy of Physics Constraint (PPC)
Ladyman & Ross already have a filter: the PPC says patterns must be compatible with physics and non-redundant. Closure *operationalizes* the PPC's vague second clause ("can't be captured at a lower level") by specifying dynamically what that means — transition autonomy such that micro-details become irrelevant. The §3.3 common-cause example is essential: a macro-variable can be PPC-compliant, projectible, and still parasitic. **Needs its own subsection.**

### 3c. Projectibility Is Necessary but Insufficient
Ladyman & Ross treat projectibility as central. You agree but show a projectible pattern can still be parasitic (tracking a common cause). Closure separates genuinely autonomous patterns from projectibly parasitic ones. This should be a key dialectical moment in the paper.

## 4. FRANKLIN & ROBERTSON AS PRIMARY INTERLOCUTOR

**Paper:** "Emerging into the Rainforest" (*European Journal for Philosophy of Science*, December 2024)

**Their criterion:** Screening-off + novelty. Two parts: conditional irrelevance (macro screens off micro-details) and unconditional relevance (macro genuinely matters for outcome). Built around *nomic macrodependencies* — law-like regularities at the macro level.

**Your criterion:** Transition autonomy under admissible intervention. Built around *transitions under perturbation*. That's a stronger demand.

**The wedge case:** §3.3 common-cause example. A variable tracking a common cause can satisfy Franklin & Robertson's conditions — it enters into robust, novel macrodependencies, it screens off micro-details. But it fails closure under intervention because the apparent autonomy was borrowed from upstream structure. The screening-off was real but parasitic.

**Tone:** Be generous, not adversarial. Their screening-off is genuinely good — it catches trout-turkeys and gerrymandered composites. The disagreement is narrow and constructive.

**Staged admittance protocol:** Dennett's compression identifies candidates → Franklin & Robertson's screening off filters gerrymanders → closure determines which survivors carry genuinely autonomous transition structure. Each step is cumulative, not competing.

---

## 5. THE LATTICE AS CENTRAL NOVEL CLAIM

Nobody has previously said: "The structure of Rainforest Realism is precisely the lattice of computationally closed coarse-grainings."

**Three metaphysical implications:**

1. **Levels aren't metaphors.** They're mathematically discoverable features of dynamical systems. Different investigators analyzing the same system converge on the same lattice because it's determined by dynamics, not representational choices.

2. **How levels relate.** Higher levels are coarse-grainings of lower levels. Each level's ε-machine is derivable from the one below. This is *nesting*, not elimination. Each level runs its own autonomous computation while being systematically related to adjacent levels.

3. **Undermines nihilism's "ground floor" assumption.** The lattice is flat with respect to causal autonomy even though hierarchical with respect to coarse-graining. The micro-level is the implementation layer, but that doesn't make it "more real."

**Graded verdicts map naturally onto lattice ontology.** Robust, qualified, indeterminate map more naturally onto a lattice than onto Dennettian "is the pattern real or not" binary. In a rainforest, you'd expect fuzzy boundaries. Your graded structure *is* that fuzziness made disciplined.

---

## 6. THE ANTI-INSTRUMENTALISM DEFENSE (currently missing from paper)

The objection "leakiness is just predictive error, not ontology" smuggles in the assumption that there's a deeper layer of Real Stuff that prediction merely tracks. But on structural realism:

- Predictive/causal relationships *are* the kind of thing reality is made of
- There's no intelligible gap between "tracks causal structure" and "is real"
- That gap only appears if you assume a God's-eye layer, which is exactly what structural realism denies
- Leakiness measures how much autonomous causal structure a partition actually has
- A high-leak partition isn't one where we fail to see the real object — it's one where the candidate genuinely lacks transition autonomy
- The prediction miss *is* the structural fact

**This should strengthen §9.1 / the instrumentalism objection response. It's the strongest anti-instrumentalism move available to the paper.**

---

## 7. THE PLATONISM BREADCRUMB (strategic decision: keep implicit)

Closure implies something larger: when achieved, you've found a structure whose identity is exhausted by its relational/transition properties, substrate-independent in exactly the way platonic objects are — except earned from physics rather than posited independently. Mathematical constructs like chess and numbers are *fully* computationally closed (their future depends only on same-level information). This explains "the platonic realm" — they're substrate-independent patterns that can be instantiated in reality.

**Strategic choice:** Leave implicit, like Dennett's original paper (nominally about Conway's Life, but everyone saw bigger implications). If stated explicitly, reviewers would spend energy on platonism debates rather than evaluating the closure criterion. Consider a single sentence in the conclusion gesturing toward it as future work.

## 12. REVISED ABSTRACT (final version from chat)

> Scale-relative structural realism holds that real patterns inhabit a multi-level ontological lattice, but the admittance criteria for this lattice remain underdetermined. Franklin and Robertson (2024) offer the most developed recent proposal: emergence, understood as screening off plus novelty, earns an entity its place in the rainforest. This paper argues that screening off is necessary but not sufficient. A macro-variable can satisfy screening off — entering into robust, novel macrodependencies that render micro-details conditionally irrelevant — while remaining parasitic on upstream structure rather than instantiating autonomous macro-dynamics. What is missing is a transition criterion. A candidate macro-object qualifies when, for a fixed regime, horizon, and admissible intervention class, macrostate information is sufficient for macro-transitions, so within-class micro-differences do not change macro-level what-follows. This closure condition catches what screening off alone misses: macro-variables that pass observational and explanatory tests but collapse under admissible intervention because their apparent autonomy was borrowed from a common cause. In exact Markov settings, strong lumpability provides a benchmark realization. In non-ideal settings, closure is graded through leakiness and convergent diagnostics under fixed constraints. The interventionist machinery is compatible with structural realism because interventions serve as diagnostic probes for relational stability, not as metaphysical primitives. The result completes rather than replaces existing admittance proposals: Dennett's compression identifies candidate patterns, Franklin and Robertson's screening off filters out gerrymandered composites, and closure determines which survivors carry genuinely autonomous transition structure. Together these provide a staged admittance protocol for rainforest ontology — with explicit downgrade conditions when closure degrades. The criterion also supplies the dynamical condition that the Putnam-Fodor autonomy tradition left implicit: multiple realizability groups realizers extensionally, while closure demands those realizers produce the same macro-transitions under declared constraints.
