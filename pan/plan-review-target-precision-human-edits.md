## Review Concern 1: Who Exactly Makes the Warrant-Transfer Move?

### Vulnerability

The paper says careful panpsychists do not claim that first-person experience alone proves panpsychism, but then criticizes a tendency to let first-person warrant travel too far. A referee may ask who actually does this.

### Current locations

- Introduction, lines 23-31: the paper distinguishes direct proof from abductive argument, then names Strawson and Goff.
- Literature positioning, line 35: Ramm is described as a more direct first-person route.
- `Goff, Strawson, and Degrees of Strain`, lines 141-157: the paper already gives Strawson, Goff, M{\o}rch, and Roelofs differentiated treatment.

### Edit plan

Add a short clarifying sentence or two in the introduction and then tighten the section on Goff and Strawson. The aim is to isolate passages where first-person authority does dialectical work, not to accuse careful writers of a simple fallacy.

Use this distinction:

> The charge is not that these authors infer panpsychism directly from pain, color, or introspective certainty. The charge is that first-person authority is sometimes asked to support a further metaphysical role: that experientiality constrains fundamental ontology, fills a Russellian categorical vacancy, or motivates an idealist-panpsychist picture.

Concrete targets:

1. Strawson: keep the existing page-cited quote, "the fundamental given natural fact" `\citep[4]{strawson2006}`. It is the strongest clean example because the text treats experience as an obligatory starting point for a realistic theory. The planned edit should say that the warrant-transfer pressure appears when this given fact is used to constrain what the fundamental physical story must include.

2. Goff: keep the Revelation discussion at `\citep[106--132]{goff2017cfr}`. Do not add a new direct quote unless the book text is verified. The planned edit should say that Revelation is useful to the panpsychist only if acquaintance does more than establish occurrence. Even if it reveals phenomenal character, it does not reveal the property's metaphysical role as fundamental, categorical, microphysical, or the occupant of a Russellian vacancy.

3. Ramm: use Ramm only as the cleanest "named target" for first-person reflection doing metaphysical work. Do not overburden the Ramm paragraph unless the exact article passage is checked. A safe sentence would be:

> Ramm is the clearest direct target here, because phenomenological reflection is explicitly used to motivate an idealist-panpsychist picture rather than merely to identify consciousness as a datum.

4. Roelofs and M{\o}rch: do not treat them as simple targets. Roelofs should remain a helpful moderation case: his medium-strength revelation thesis shows that constitutive facts need not be transparently available in introspection. M{\o}rch should remain a constructive-theory case: phenomenal powers may do real explanatory work, but that work is theoretical.

### Suggested insertion point

After line 31 or in the first paragraph of Section 7, add one compact paragraph:

> These examples should be read as locating a pressure point, not as attributing a crude inference. In Strawson, the pressure appears when the given reality of experience constrains what a realistic physicalism may be. In Goff, it appears when Revelation helps move from the character of experience to claims about the nature that can occupy the Russellian role. In Ramm, it appears more directly, because first-person phenomenological reflection is used to motivate an idealist-panpsychist picture. In each case, the question is not whether experience matters. It is whether its first-person authority reaches the metaphysical role then assigned to it.

This is a small addition, but it answers the referee's "who exactly?" question.

## Review Concern 2: Structure Versus Existence

### Vulnerability

The paper argues that once organization explains the structure of ordinary consciousness, the hidden phenomenal base has less work to do. A panpsychist may reply that organization explains structure, while the hidden phenomenal base explains why there is anything it is like at all.

### Current locations

- `Explanatory Burden`, lines 169-173.
- Conclusion, lines 191-195.

### Edit plan

Make the distinction explicit before pressing the burden. The paper should not sound as if organizational explanation by itself solves the hard problem. It should say that the argument targets the contribution of the hidden base to the determinate form of ordinary consciousness, while granting that panpsychists may assign the hidden base a role in explaining phenomenal existence.

Replace or lightly revise the opening of line 171 with a more precise distinction:

> There are two explanatory tasks here. One is to explain why there is experience at all. The other is to explain why ordinary experience has the unity, temporal grain, valence, perspectival closure, and access profile it has. Panpsychists may say that the hidden phenomenal base is meant chiefly for the first task. The present pressure concerns the second task and the relation between the two.

Then keep the existing burden:

> If organization explains the determinate structure of ordinary consciousness, the hidden base must still explain what it contributes to that structure, or why an organized non-phenomenal base would not leave the same structural story in place.

### Guardrails

- Do not claim that organization explains phenomenal existence.
- Do not imply that panpsychism has no role left. Say it may have a serious metaphysical role in blocking brute emergence.
- Do not let the paragraph become a full hard-problem discussion. The paper's point is narrower: the hidden base cannot inherit first-person warrant, so its explanatory role must be specified.

## Review Concern 3: Pure Awareness and Private Acquaintance

### Vulnerability

The reportability constraint works against public evidential use of pure-awareness reports. But a sympathetic reader may say the report is not the evidence. The private acquaintance is the evidence.

### Current locations

- `The Reportability Constraint`, lines 93-103.
- The strongest current sentence is line 97: "A panpsychist might say that private acquaintance with pure awareness need not be report-mediated. But then the original dilemma returns..."

### Edit plan

Make that reply more explicit and less compressed. The paper should grant the private-acquaintance point, then separate private warrant from public dialectical warrant.

Suggested replacement for the last two sentences of line 97:

> A panpsychist might say that private acquaintance with pure awareness need not be report-mediated. That point should be granted. If such acquaintance occurs, it may have whatever first-person force it has for the subject. But the moment it is used as a public reason for panpsychism, it reaches the argument through memory, description, interpretation, and classification. More importantly, the private appeal still faces the original dilemma: either the state is given with some phenomenal character, however attenuated, or it is not disclosed as experience in the relevant first-person sense.

Then tighten line 103 slightly so the conclusion does not repeat the same thought mechanically:

> Pure-awareness cases therefore do not bypass the dilemma. They either provide evidence for a very thin phenomenal character, or they do not provide first-person evidence for experience at all.

### Guardrails

- Do not deny that pure-awareness or cessation experiences occur.
- Do not say that private experience is worthless because it is private.
- Do say that public philosophical use requires public argumentative handling.
- Keep Lin, Windt, Zahavi, Henry and Thompson, Metzinger, and Albahari citations intact.

1. "The paper never identifies who makes the warrant-transfer move."
