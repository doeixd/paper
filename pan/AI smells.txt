Negation structures are a major AI smell: “not just X, but Y,” “isn’t merely X,” “no longer X, but Y,” “less about X than Y.”
Subtle versions count too: “on paper X, in reality Y,” “the promise is X, the result is Y,” “what looks like X is really Y.”
The smell is the correction rhythm: the prose keeps denying one idea, then revealing the “real” idea.
It can make simple claims sound fake-profound: every sentence becomes a reframe, pivot, or miniature TED Talk line.
A big issue is that of restatement, or announcement.
