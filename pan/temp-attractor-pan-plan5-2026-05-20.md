# Attractor exercise for pan_plan5 corrective pass

Temporary workspace following `AI_ATTRACTOR_EDITING_METHOD.md`. This file is observational. It records high-convergence passages, nearby variants, the repeated attractor state, and the conservative edits to make in the manuscript.

## Quick pattern scan

High-frequency / high-convergence phrases in the current manuscript:

- "does not" / "do not": necessary for the argument, but it clusters around warrant limits and can create a correction rhythm.
- "first-person datum": useful, but repeated enough that some sentences begin to sound like thesis restatement.
- "hidden phenomenal base" / "hidden phenomenal posits": necessary, but should vary with "theoretical base," "these posits," or the specific posit where possible.
- "theoretical": necessary, but repeated as a closer: "its warrant is theoretical," "must be defended by theory," "permissible as theory."
- "The question is" family: mostly avoided, but "The live question is therefore one of reach" remains an announcement-like seam.
- "not X, but Y" / "not..., but..." family: several are load-bearing, but some can be softened into direct claims.
- short factual stacking: "It is X. It does Y. It does not Z." appears at seams after the restructure.

The repeated model attractor is:

> concession -> limit -> theoretical-status closer.

The shape is accurate for this paper, but too many paragraphs close by saying some version of "this may still be defended, but only as theory." The fix should not remove the defense. It should make a few sentences carry more specific content instead of ending in the same theoretical-status formula.

## Target 1: datum handoff

Original:

> The live question is therefore one of reach. First-person experience is indispensable evidence for consciousness. It does not automatically support claims about micro-experience, proto-experience, proto-phenomenal categorical bases, cosmic subjectivity, or phenomenal bonding relations.

Variants:

1. The dispute begins when that authority is extended beyond the experience that supplies it.
2. The issue is reach: the same evidence that establishes consciousness does not by itself establish micro-experience or cosmic subjectivity.
3. First-person evidence is indispensable for consciousness; the dispute starts when it is asked to underwrite what it does not present.
4. The datum has authority, but the authority runs only as far as the experience disclosed.
5. Consciousness is not in doubt here. The dispute concerns the extra reach claimed for acquaintance.
6. First-person evidence gives consciousness; it does not give the hidden structures panpsychism may introduce.
7. The problem starts at the extension from given consciousness to unacquainted phenomenal structure.
8. The authority is real. Its reach is limited.
9. Experience gives evidence for consciousness. It does not automatically give evidence for micro-experience, proto-experience, cosmic subjectivity, or bonding relations.
10. The live issue is how far that authority travels.

Attractor:

- "The issue/question/problem is..." announcement.
- Two short sentences followed by a list.
- Motto-like "authority is real, reach is limited."

Conservative direction:

Use variant 3 with concrete add-on. It keeps the logic but avoids "the live question is."

Planned edit:

> First-person evidence is indispensable for consciousness; the dispute starts when it is asked to underwrite what it does not present: micro-experience, proto-experience, proto-phenomenal categorical bases, cosmic subjectivity, or phenomenal bonding relations.

## Target 2: constraint modesty

Original:

> The constraint is modest. It allows theoretical posits. Physics, biology, and psychology all legitimately posit entities and structures not given in experience. Those posits are warranted by explanatory, predictive, or unificatory work. They do not inherit the authority of first-person acquaintance.

Variants:

1. The constraint is modest because it leaves room for theoretical posits.
2. Nothing in the constraint bans theoretical entities.
3. Physics, biology, and psychology posit entities not given in experience; the point is how those posits are warranted.
4. The constraint leaves ordinary theoretical practice intact.
5. Theoretical posits are legitimate when explanatory, predictive, or unificatory work supports them.
6. The constraint does not make acquaintance the only route to warrant.
7. It blocks inherited first-person authority, not theoretical positing.
8. The problem is not positing hidden structure. The problem is claiming the wrong kind of warrant for it.
9. A posit can be legitimate without being given in acquaintance.
10. The relevant contrast is between earned theoretical support and inherited first-person authority.

Attractor:

- "The constraint is modest. It allows..." is useful but stacked.
- "not X, but Y" versions appear immediately.
- "The relevant contrast is..." sounds like model narration.

Conservative direction:

Merge the first two sentences and keep the concrete scientific comparison.

Planned edit:

> The constraint is modest because it leaves room for theoretical posits. Physics, biology, and psychology all legitimately posit entities and structures not given in experience; those posits are warranted by explanatory, predictive, or unificatory work rather than by inherited first-person authority.

## Target 3: Ramm direct target

Original:

> Ramm's panpsychist idealism is the most direct first-person target, since it begins from the first-person perspective and uses phenomenological reflection to motivate an idealist-panpsychist picture. Such reflection may have metaphysical relevance, but the metaphysical step still has to be marked as a step; once first-person reflection is asked to support idealist or panpsychist structure beyond what experience itself discloses, its warrant has become theoretical.

Variants:

1. Ramm gives the direct case because first-person reflection is explicitly asked to do metaphysical work.
2. Ramm is the cleanest target here: phenomenological reflection is not merely evidence for experience but part of the route to an idealist-panpsychist metaphysics.
3. In Ramm's case, the first-person starting point is not incidental; it is part of the route to the metaphysical view.
4. Ramm's panpsychist idealism matters because it makes the first-person route explicit.
5. The issue is not whether phenomenological reflection matters. The issue is when it is asked to support what experience does not disclose.
6. Such reflection may matter metaphysically. It still changes status when it supports idealist or panpsychist structure beyond what experience gives.
7. Ramm therefore supplies a useful test case: once reflection reaches beyond the disclosed episode, its warrant is no longer simple acquaintance.
8. The argument need not deny the relevance of reflection. It only requires the transition to theory to be visible.
9. Ramm's case makes visible the step the paper is tracking.
10. First-person reflection can begin the route; it cannot make every later metaphysical structure first-personally given.

Attractor:

- "The issue is not X. The issue is Y."
- "The argument need not deny..."
- "test case" language is useful but can become mechanical.
- Ending with "warrant is theoretical" repeats the paper's standard closer.

Conservative direction:

Use the specificity of variants 1, 3, and 10. Keep the defense but avoid the generic "may have relevance, but..." rhythm.

Planned edit:

> Ramm is the cleanest direct case because first-person reflection is explicitly asked to do metaphysical work. In that argument, the first-person starting point is part of the route to an idealist-panpsychist picture. Reflection can begin such a route; it cannot make every later metaphysical structure first-personally given.

## Target 4: pure awareness opener

Original:

> The strongest challenge here comes from meditators who report pure or contentless awareness: moments of consciousness without object, self, or thought, which may seem to approach the bare subjectivity the preceding argument denies is given in experience. The challenge can be granted without granting its conclusion.

Variants:

1. Pure-awareness reports are the hardest case for the abstraction argument.
2. Meditative reports of contentless awareness press the argument at its weakest-looking point.
3. Reports of awareness without object, self, or thought seem to offer exactly the kind of minimal datum the abstraction argument resists.
4. The pure-awareness literature supplies the strongest pressure on this point.
5. The challenge should be taken seriously: some reports describe consciousness without object, self, or thought.
6. These reports do not need to be dismissed.
7. The paper can grant the reports' seriousness while denying that they establish bare metaphysical subjectivity.
8. The difficult case is not rich waking consciousness but reported awareness with most familiar structure suspended.
9. A meditative report of awareness without object, self, or thought looks close to bare subjectivity.
10. That appearance is enough to make the case worth granting for argument's sake.

Attractor:

- "The strongest challenge..." appears often in academic prose, but here it is acceptable.
- "grant without granting" is a repeated concessive pattern.
- "which may seem to approach" is a little buffered but fair.

Conservative direction:

Use a more concrete first sentence and then grant. Avoid "challenge can be granted without granting its conclusion."

Planned edit:

> The difficult case is reported awareness with most familiar structure suspended: consciousness without object, self, or thought. These reports should not be dismissed. They may seem to approach the bare subjectivity the preceding argument denies is given in experience.

## Target 5: Schwitzgebel / examples closer

Original:

> These examples illustrate the warrant problem; they are not empirical proofs against panpsychism. Panpsychism repeatedly posits experience or experience-like properties where experience itself gives no clear warrant. That may be permissible as theory, but it is not directly licensed by the first-person datum. Schwitzgebel's worries about introspective unreliability strengthen the restriction: if introspection is fallible even within its proper domain, then its extension to hidden micro-, proto-, or cosmic phenomenal structure requires special caution.

Variants:

1. The examples are illustrations, not empirical proofs against panpsychism.
2. They show the pattern: experience-like properties are placed where experience itself gives no clear warrant.
3. Such placement may be theoretically legitimate; it is not licensed by acquaintance alone.
4. Schwitzgebel's worries about introspection add pressure, since fallibility inside the proper domain makes extensions outside it especially risky.
5. The examples do not decide the metaphysics. They show where warrant has changed kind.
6. The panpsychist can still posit hidden experientiality. The warrant for doing so must come from theory.
7. If introspection is unreliable even near home, it should not be casually extended to hidden micro-, proto-, or cosmic structure.
8. These illustrations leave panpsychism open, while denying it the first-person shortcut.
9. The conclusion is epistemic rather than empirical.
10. The point is about license, not possibility.

Attractor:

- "not empirical proofs" is necessary but close to "not X, but Y."
- "may be permissible as theory, but..." repeats the concession-limitation rhythm.
- "the point is..." variants are especially generic.

Conservative direction:

Keep the illustration/proof caveat, then replace the generic "permissible as theory" with "the metaphysics remains open."

Planned edit:

> These examples illustrate the warrant problem; they are not empirical proofs against panpsychism. The metaphysics remains open. What the examples show is where warrant has changed kind: experience-like properties are placed where experience itself gives no clear warrant. Schwitzgebel's worries about introspective unreliability strengthen the restriction, since fallibility within introspection's proper domain makes extensions to hidden micro-, proto-, or cosmic phenomenal structure especially risky.

## Exercise result

Patterns to avoid in the next patch:

- "the question/issue/point is" announcement.
- "may still be true, but..." concession rhythm.
- final sentence as "therefore theoretical."
- stacked short sentences at section seams.
- repeated use of "first-person datum" where "experience," "acquaintance," or a specific example can do the work.

Patch priorities:

1. Replace the datum handoff.
2. Merge the constraint-modesty stack.
3. Recast Ramm paragraph around a direct target, not a generic concession.
4. Make pure-awareness opener less over-ascriptive and less "grant without granting."
5. Recast Schwitzgebel/examples closer away from "permissible as theory, but..."

## Final applied deviations from the planned edits

- The combination sentence was revised again after the pattern scan. Instead of "My question is prior," the manuscript now says "The prior issue is warrant," avoiding the "question is" family while preserving the order-of-warrant point.
- The pure-awareness opener keeps the seriousness of the reports but folds the concession into an add-on sentence: "These reports should not be dismissed, since..."
- The Schwitzgebel closer now says "They show a change in kind of warrant," avoiding the more generic "What the examples show is..."
