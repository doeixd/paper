# Summary of Panpsychism Textual Targeting Revisions

## Changes made

- Added a short passage in the scope section identifying real textual targets for the warrant-transfer concern: Strawson's treatment of experience as a fundamental given and Goff's Revelation thesis.
- Revised the Russellian section to explain why the phenomenal filling move is tempting before rejecting it as underlicensed by first-person evidence alone.
- Expanded the temporal grain illustration so it now asks what hidden micro-experiential facts explain about thresholds, temporal boundaries, and integration.
- Strengthened the Schwitzgebel point by clarifying that introspective unreliability compounds, rather than competes with, the paper's warrant-limiting argument.

## Rationale

The review pressure was that the paper might look as if it targets only a crude panpsychist inference that sophisticated panpsychists would reject. The new passage makes the target more concrete without overstating it: Strawson and Goff are treated as careful philosophers, but their arguments still draw force from the special epistemic standing of experience. That lets the paper ask how far that standing can legitimately travel.

The Russellian revision improves charity. It now grants that phenomenal filling is natural because consciousness seems to be the one candidate for intrinsic nature available from the inside. The objection is therefore not that the move is arbitrary, but that it remains a metaphysical inference rather than a deliverance of first-person acquaintance.

The temporal grain revision keeps the example instead of cutting it, but gives it a clearer role. It no longer gestures vaguely at time thresholds. It now presses the explanatory question: if hidden experientiality is placed beneath conscious temporal structure, what does it explain about that structure?
