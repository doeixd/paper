# Summary of Panpsychism Draft Argument Sharpening

Revised the initial panpsychism LaTeX draft to address the main weaknesses identified in the review against `pan_plan.txt` and `pan_notes.txt`.

Changes made:
- Added a direct response to appeals to phenomenal acquaintance: acquaintance may support claims about the experience one is acquainted with, but it does not automatically extend to micro-experience, proto-phenomenal categorical bases, cosmic subjectivity, or subject-derivation relations.
- Restored the hard-problem concession from the notes. The paper now explicitly says it is not deriving phenomenality from organization or claiming to solve the hard problem.
- Strengthened the explanatory-burden section by clarifying that panpsychism relocates, rather than removes, the difficulty unless hidden phenomenal properties explain the specific unity, content, temporal flow, attention, memory, and valence of ordinary consciousness.
- Neutralized several rhetorically loaded phrases so the paper remains charitable toward sophisticated panpsychist views.

Validation performed:
- Rebuilt successfully with `pdflatex -> bibtex -> pdflatex -> pdflatex` from `pan/`.
- Re-ran a style scan for em dashes, footnote references, and the previously flagged loaded terms.
- Removed temporary LaTeX build artifacts after validation.
