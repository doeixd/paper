# Summary of Edits

Created four new detailed source summaries in `sources/detailed-source-summaries/`:

- `37-franklinrobertson2024.md`
- `38-jiang2024.md`
- `39-jiang2025.md`
- `40-ladymanlorenzetti2024.md`

## What Changed
Each file was written in the existing house style used in nearby source summaries. All four include:

- a top key heading
- `## Full Citation`
- `## Detailed Summary`
- `## Core Argument Map`
- `## Relation to Real Patterns Need Closure`
- `## Reviewer-Relevant Cautions`
- `## Text Source`

The summaries were based directly on the corresponding text-converted source files, with attention to each paper's central thesis, argumentative structure, examples, and stated limits.

## Why
The goal was to expand the source-summary library with accurate, paper-specific reference files that can be used later in drafting and citation work without overstating relevance to `real-patterns-need-closure`.

I kept the RPNC relation sections calibrated. In each case, I identified genuine points of overlap while avoiding the stronger claim that these papers already defend a closure criterion.

## Editorial Notes
- Franklin and Robertson were summarized as a close neighbouring view focused on emergence via novelty plus screening off.
- Jiang 2024 was treated mainly as structuralist background on mechanisms, with limited and programmatic relevance to real patterns.
- Jiang 2025 was treated as a recent processualist-effective-realist reconciliation using real-patterns language, but with a deliberately cautious note about permissiveness.
- Ladyman and Lorenzetti were treated as a key contemporary effective-realist structuralist source, especially on modal structure and scale relativity.

## Cautions
- Two of the sources were preprint-derived text conversions, so the summaries reflect the converted source text and may differ slightly in wording from final published copy.
- No claims were added beyond what the source texts clearly support.
