## Rationale

The original outline captured the basic idea, but it needed a clearer research path and more guidance for turning the idea into a publishable article. The expanded version now shows how to connect the proposed argument to acquaintance theory, revelation debates, phenomenal concept strategies, introspective skepticism, and philosophy of science work on theoretical posits without letting any one literature take over the paper.

The main strategic point remains: the follow-up paper should not become a general critique of panpsychism or introspection. It should argue that once a theory moves from acquaintance to hidden ontology, the warrant changes kind and must be assessed as theoretical or abductive warrant.
