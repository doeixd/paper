## Changes by item

### 1. Headline verb: "completes" → "sharpens"

Two occurrences touched:

- Introduction (near line 58): "The aim is to sharpen rainforest realism by adding the dynamical downgrade condition its prior filters do not yet supply." Previously "complete rainforest realism by supplying the missing dynamical downgrade condition." The change drops the overclaiming "completes/missing" framing while keeping the substantive claim that prior filters do not by themselves supply this condition.
- Conclusion opening (near line 2222): "The view defended here sharpens the cumulative admittance protocol ... by adding a closure filter." Previously "completes a cumulative admittance protocol ... by adding a final closure filter." Drops "final" along with "completes"; the criterion is positioned as a filter within the protocol rather than its capstone.

Other uses of "complete/completes" (e.g. "not a complete metaphysics of levels" in the conclusion) left untouched — those are scope disclaimers and should stay.

### 2. Failure-mode vocabulary unified around "parasitic"

Three near-labels were drifting in the prior text: "parasitic," "borrowed autonomy," and "high-maintenance." Resolution:

- "parasitic" retained as the single primary label. Its definition block at lines 82–84 is preserved intact.
- "borrowed autonomy" never appeared as a standalone label in the prior text (it functioned only as a phrase within the definition of "parasitic"). Confirmed by grep: zero remaining occurrences of "borrowed autonomy" as a noun phrase.
- "high-maintenance" narrowed to its technical home in §3 (Minimal Formal Illustration, line 942 — where it is defined — and line 1544 where it is referenced back). The prior use at the head of §5.4 Objections ("high-maintenance, transition-incoherent candidates") replaced with "parasitic and transition-incoherent candidates," so the primary label and the other distinct failure type both appear without introducing a third quasi-label.

"Gerrymandered" retained as an established near-synonym (glossed at lines 78–80 as "dynamically incoherent"). Its footprint was not expanded.

### 3. "Transition autonomy" given one compact canonical definition

Added a single labeled sentence at first introduction (new line in the definition block): "Throughout this paper, \emph{transition autonomy} names the property the criterion tests: the candidate partition's own dynamics close under admissible perturbation, so within-class micro-detail adds no predictive power." Later uses of the term are now bare references, not re-explanations. Most of the passages initially flagged as redescriptions turned out on closer reading to be thesis statements or label-uses, which were left untouched.

### 7. Conclusion scope disclaimers consolidated

The scattered disclaimers were consolidated into a single explicit paragraph beginning "It is worth stating plainly what this paper does not claim." The paragraph lists: no complete metaphysics of levels (scope restricted to induced closure in spatiotemporal systems under declared constraints); no settlement of general emergence/reduction debates; no universal implementation recipe (domain-specific estimator choices, robustness checks, and control-channel inventories remain downstream and do not alter the philosophical content); strongest conclusion conditional on structural-realist + interventionist commitments, with a narrower anti-gerrymandering result available without them.

The final paragraph retains the forward-looking material: future cross-domain testing, explicit-commitments-over-silence claim, and the substrate-independence question.

## Self-review and defensive restorations

After the initial cut pass, a self-review found three places where compression had weakened defensibility. All were restored before the build:

1. **Non-Markovian lumpability caveat** in §3: "That does not trivialize non-Markovian cases or the formal difficulty of establishing lumpability in them." Pre-empts the objection that the Markov framing is being used to dismiss hard cases.

2. **Regime-parasitism link and operational stabilization definition** in §2.5: the sentence tying the refusal-of-robust-standing to parasitism-on-regime-characterization; the gloss of "stabilization" as ordering that stops shifting under defensible protocol variations and does not presuppose a privileged grain. Both matter for the objection replies in §5.

3. **Bounded-agent operational gloss** on Pearl's $do(x)$: restored "interventions no bounded agent could implement without destroying the target regime" so "physically unrealizable" is not read loosely.

No qualifications, caveats, or regime-indexing were dropped in the final state. No "conditional on" or "under declared constraints" qualifier was silently upgraded to an unconditional claim. The central move is a verb swap plus vocabulary consolidation plus placement shifts plus rebalancing — not a thesis change.
