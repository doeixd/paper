## Summary

Audited the active Synthese manuscript against the source-summary corpus and tightened a set of source uses that were accurate in direction but too broad, too compressed, or more strongly stated than the cited sources warranted.

Primary file revised:

- `latex/real-patterns-need-closure-SYNTHESE-mainbody.tex`

## What Changed

Adjusted the Franklin and Robertson framing so the paper now tracks their novelty condition more closely. The manuscript no longer describes their view as requiring a vaguely stronger macro-level novelty claim. It now states that the relevant macro-level dependency profile is not type-identical to the realizing micro-dependencies, which better fits both the paper and its compatibility with reducibility.

Separated the paper's intervention-diagnostic move from Ladyman and Lorenzetti's own claims. Their citation is now used for scale-relative, regime-dependent but realist ontology, while the interventional diagnostic is stated explicitly as this paper's addition rather than implicitly attributed to them.

Cleaned up the literature-positioning sentence in the introduction. The source clusters are now more accurate:

- Simon and Ando for near-decomposability and aggregation
- Wimsatt for non-aggregativity and reductionist bias
- Machamer and Craver for mechanistic organization
- Rosas for formal closure diagnostics
- Hoel and Dewhurst for causal-emergence debate
- Millhouse, Wallace, Jiang, and Ladyman for recent pattern-realist and adjacent structuralist discussion

Removed an overstatement about Batterman. The manuscript no longer equates universality classes with closure. It now presents Batterman more cautiously as showing why stable macro-behavior can persist across wide micro-variation, while closure is added as a further objecthood discipline.

Split support between Shalizi and Rosas in the computational-mechanics discussion. The manuscript now cites:

- `shalizi2001` for the `\varepsilon`-machine
- `rosas2024` for the `\upsilon`-machine and closure comparison

Refined the Hoel citation so it no longer reduces effective-information results to simple gains in determinism. The text now refers more accurately to macro-level increases in causal informativeness through changes in determinism and degeneracy.

Substantially narrowed the Landauer paragraph. `landauer1961` is now used only for the modest but important point that information processing is physically non-free. The stronger claims about evolution or self-organization selecting low-leakage partitions were removed as source-supported claims and replaced by a weaker philosophical inference stated in the paper's own voice.

Narrowed the Ross citation. The manuscript now cites `ross1995` only for the claim that abstract strategic patterns can be real empirical objects without reduction to individual-level ontology. The extension to institutional kinds is now explicitly presented as this paper's own development rather than as something Ross already establishes.

Qualified the later Franklin and Robertson discussion by adding that their screening-off condition can hold exactly or approximately, and by restating the novelty side in terms closer to their own formulation.

Separated Machamer and Craver more cleanly in the mechanisms discussion. Machamer now supports productive organization, while Craver carries the interventionist and explanatory-stability emphasis.

Narrowed the Boyd citation so it now supports leakiness claims about biological kinds rather than being stretched across biological, psychological, and economic domains all at once.

Fixed a minor but real Andersen issue by correcting the pronoun and leaving the density-of-structure point intact.

Softened the Woodward comparison. The paper no longer attributes a strong proportionality result to `woodward2021`; instead it presents closure as a dynamical analogue to Woodward's graded invariance point.

Softened the conclusion's literature-compression sentence so it no longer says the cited views all leave open exactly the same thing in the same way. It now says they do not by themselves yet supply the paper's specific closure-based admittance and downgrade discipline.

## Why

The main goal was not to change the paper's thesis, but to make each citation do exactly the amount of argumentative work the underlying source can defensibly bear. Several sources were already being used in the right neighborhood, but the prose occasionally compressed distinctions that matter to a skeptical reviewer. Those distinctions are load-bearing because the paper's argumentative posture depends on showing that closure is an additional discipline, not something already fully present in neighboring accounts.

## Residual Notes

- `boyd1991` is still doing fairly limited work and may be worth replacing or supplementing later if the objection section grows more domain-specific.
- `millhouseetal2026` is no longer cited in the main positioning sentence after the tightening pass and remains absent from the summary corpus because no local source text has been added for it.
- I did not attempt a full rewrite of all citation framing. The revisions were targeted to passages where the source summaries suggested the clearest overreach or compression risk.
