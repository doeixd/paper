# Summary of Edits: Pan Source Verification Plan

Created and corrected the source-integration plan for the `pan/` paper after a stricter verification pass.

## What changed

- Added an explicit verification-status section to `pan/source-integration-plan.md`.
- Clarified that Alter's 2023 book is best treated as a recent route from the knowledge argument to Russellian monism, not simply as a standalone structure/dynamics argument.
- Clarified that Pereboom should be used to show Russellian monism's place within physicalist dialectics, not treated as a panpsychist target in the same sense as Goff or Strawson.
- Clarified Balog's publication-date issue: Rutgers lists the article as January 2012, while some PhilPapers metadata lists 2011. The plan recommends using 2012 to match the journal issue record.
- Corrected the optional Loar recommendation from the less precise 1997 anthology reference to the stable 1990 *Philosophical Perspectives* article.
- Reframed Carruthers as optional for a compact parenthetical acknowledgment of the broader phenomenal-concept literature, rather than as a worked interlocutor.

## Rationale

The purpose of the plan is to strengthen the manuscript without expanding it into a general literature review. The verification pass protects against two reviewer-facing risks: mischaracterizing a major source and adding citations that do not actually serve the paper's narrow warrant argument. The corrections keep the manuscript's likely integration strategy modest, accurate, and defensible.

## Verification basis

The bibliographic and interpretive checks used primary Oxford Academic pages for Alter, Pereboom, and Stoljar, the Rutgers publication record for Balog, and stable scholarly metadata for Loar.
