# 2026-03-18 — Convergence Reframe: The Paper's Real Contribution

## Summary

Major reframe of the paper's self-presentation. The paper was advertising brittleness diagnostics as its primary novelty, which led reviewers to ask "what does brittleness add beyond failed prediction?" The actual philosophical contribution — that Quine's architecture at scale *entails* convergence on objective relational structure, and that coherentism under shared constraints *is* the mechanism that produces structural realism — was buried under diagnostic machinery. This edit surfaces the real contribution in all framing sections.

## Core Reframe

- FROM: "We add brittleness diagnostics to Quine → this drives convergence toward the Apex Network"
- TO: "Quine's architecture at scale entails convergence on real structure → brittleness names what this process looks like from above"

Brittleness is honestly demoted from "the novel contribution" to "a useful descriptive aggregate of the Quinean revision process operating at scale." The philosophical contribution is the convergence claim itself and the synthesis: coherentism and structural realism are not rivals but two descriptions of the same process at different scales.
