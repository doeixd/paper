# Summary of Panpsychism Recent Literature Engagement Revisions

This revision strengthens the paper against the reviewer-style objection that the argument targets a tendency rather than live panpsychist positions.

## Changes Made

- Added direct engagement with Roelofs's revelation defense and Lin's recent challenge to revelation-based arguments for panpsychism.
- Added Ramm as a named first-person route to panpsychist idealism, which gives the paper a clearer textual target for the warrant-transfer concern.
- Corrected the depsychologization citation: the relevant article is by Keith Frankish, not Howard Robinson.
- Added Frankish's depsychologization point to support the section on bare or thinned-out subjectivity, while avoiding reliance on Frankish's illusionist commitments.
- Added Mørch's phenomenal powers view as a test case for the paper's constraint: if phenomenal powers explain physical dispositions, that explanatory role must be earned theoretically rather than imported directly from ordinary acquaintance.
- Expanded the cosmopsychism discussion with Shani, Albahari, Mørch, and Goff's recent hybrid cosmopsychism, so the paper no longer treats cosmopsychism as a simple mirror image of micropsychist combination.
- Added Mendelovici and Saad to the explanatory-burden section to acknowledge two strong panpsychist replies: that combination-style problems are not unique to panpsychism, and that panpsychism may be motivated by abductive routes beyond first-person evidence.
- Updated the panpsychism BibTeX database and the root Chicago-style `references.md` entries for the new sources.

## Rationale

The main goal was to preserve the paper's central claim while making it harder for a referee to dismiss the target as under-specified. The paper still does not claim to refute panpsychism. It now more explicitly argues that first-person evidence gives panpsychism a starting datum, but not a transferable license for hidden micro-, proto-, or cosmic phenomenal ontology. Where panpsychism is defended through revelation, phenomenal powers, composite subjectivity, harmony, or cosmopsychic individuation, its warrant must be evaluated as theoretical and abductive.

This also keeps the paper aligned with the style guide: the prose remains cautious, the argumentative scope is qualified, and the added literature is used to clarify the dialectic rather than to create a broad survey for its own sake.
