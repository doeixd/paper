# Summary of Edits: Pan Surgical Polish After Review

Date: 2026-05-20 08:53

Edited `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex` and refreshed `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-sn.pdf`.

## What changed

- Replaced a remaining review-response phrase with a more direct target-precision sentence about Strawson, Goff, and Ramm.
- Softened several uses of "ordinary consciousness" where the phrase could make unusual states look excluded from the datum.
- Revised the numbered warrant argument so the datum is described as consciousness at the grain of acquaintance, with phenomenal character however attenuated.
- Recast the primitivist reply so the paper does not rely on the broad claim that all determinate phenomenal character demands reductive explanation.
- Qualified the Goff 2024 discussion so the pressure is that anti-emergentist motivation narrows around subjecthood, not that the view collapses.
- Lightly compressed the pure-awareness paragraph to reduce sentence stacking and review-response rhythm.

## Rationale

The edits protect the paper from avoidable objections while preserving the central thesis. The paper should not imply that minimal, dreamless, objectless, or pure-awareness cases fall outside the first-person datum. It also should not invite a primitivist objection by making the argument depend on a general demand that all determinacy be reductively explained. The revised prose keeps the focus on warrant: acquaintance with experience does not itself license hidden distribution, fundamentality, microphysical placement, cosmic subjectivity, or phenomenal bonding.
