# Summary of Edits: Pan Voice Integration Pass

## File Edited

- `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex`

## Purpose

This pass revised the mainbody draft for voice, integration, and prose rhythm. The goal was not to change the argument, add new claims, or make the paper more defensive. It was to make the existing argument read more like a polished philosophy paper: clear, measured, simple where possible, and less marked by revision scaffolding.

## Main Changes

1. **Reduced formulaic signposting.** The introduction and conclusion now state the paper's limited result more directly, without announcing later sections or over-explaining the argumentative payoff.

2. **Smoothed the abductive concession.** The scope section now handles the serious panpsychist reply more cleanly: careful panpsychism may be abductive, but once it is abductive its hidden phenomenal ontology must be judged as theory rather than as directly first-personal evidence.

3. **Trimmed over-defensive prose.** Several paragraphs that repeated the same warrant boundary were tightened. The revision keeps the qualifications, especially that panpsychism is not refuted and that theoretical posits remain legitimate, but removes unnecessary repetition.

4. **Simplified the abstraction reply.** The mass analogy discussion was shortened and made more elegant. It still blocks the objection that the paper forbids legitimate abstraction, but it now does so without an extended sequence of sub-distinctions.

5. **Improved transitions and paragraph flow.** The revised text better connects first-person warrant, hidden ontology, Russellian vacancy, combination, and explanatory burden. The paper now moves more smoothly from the central constraint to its applications.

6. **Calibrated tone.** Informal or overly rhetorical phrases such as "earn its keep," "doing the paying," and "rhetorical glow" were removed or replaced. The revised prose is more restrained and more suitable for peer review.

## Rationale

The paper's main risk was not a weak thesis but an overly visible defensive architecture. The draft had accumulated language that told the reader what each move was doing instead of letting the sequence of claims carry the argument. This pass preserves the core distinctions while making the prose less mechanical. It also keeps the paper's humility: panpsychism remains a live metaphysical option, but it cannot transfer the authority of ordinary first-person consciousness to hidden phenomenal posits without further argument.
