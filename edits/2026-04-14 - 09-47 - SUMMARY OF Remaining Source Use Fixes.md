## What Changed

Qualified the Woodward/Pearl/Hoel bundle in the intervention discussion. The manuscript now distinguishes:

- Woodward and Pearl for the interventionist framework
- Hoel for a conditional route by which macro-variables can be more causally informative than micro-descriptions

Rewrote the multiple-realizability comparison so it no longer treats closure as what Putnam, Fodor, and Shapiro had already shown. The paragraph now presents multiple realizability as a natural consequence of closure in relevant cases, not as a sufficient or independently decisive test, and uses Shapiro for the narrower point that heterogeneous realization alone does not secure explanatory autonomy.

## Why

These remaining changes were mainly about preventing the manuscript from attributing too much to neighboring literatures. The core argument depends on showing that closure is a further discipline added to existing debates, not a conclusion already established by them. Tightening those passages reduces reviewer exposure on precisely that point.
