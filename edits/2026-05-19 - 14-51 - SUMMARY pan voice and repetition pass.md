# Summary of Edits: Panpsychism Voice and Repetition Pass

This pass made small prose-level revisions to `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex` to reduce formulaic repetition without cutting the paper's reviewer-facing qualifications.

The main concern was that several passages repeated the same concession pattern: panpsychism may still be true, but its hidden phenomenal posits must be defended as theory rather than inherited from first-person acquaintance. That point is central and was preserved, but the wording now varies more naturally across the introduction, scope section, warrant-transfer section, and conclusion.

Specific changes:

- Replaced the blunt introductory concession, "Panpsychism may still be true," with "Panpsychism remains a live metaphysical option," preserving the concession while avoiding a repeated disclaimer rhythm.
- Smoothed the opening of the Scope and Bracketing section by removing exposed signposts such as "The scope is limited," "My target is narrower," and "The argument is not aimed at panpsychism as such." The same limitations remain, but they now read as part of the argumentative setup rather than as defensive announcements.
- Rephrased the warrant-transfer closing paragraph so that the key claim is less formulaic: panpsychism overdraws when it lets the authority of ordinary experience travel beyond what experience itself discloses.
- Replaced another repeated "may still be true" construction in the Goff/Strawson discussion with a plainer statement that the relevant machinery can still be defended, but stands on theoretical footing once detached from acquaintance.
- Removed one redundant "The claim is narrower" sentence in the conclusion while preserving the point that illusionism and deflationary physicalism face their own burdens.

A temporary drafting note, `pan/temp-voice-repetition-rephrases.md`, was created to generate alternate phrasings and identify the attractor state to avoid. The manuscript changes draw only from the conservative options in that note.

No philosophical commitments were changed. The paper still brackets full Russellian monism, allows independent abductive defenses of panpsychism, preserves the Lin/Roelofs framing, and keeps the core distinction between consciousness as a datum and hidden phenomenal ontology as a theoretical posit.
