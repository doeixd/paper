# Summary of Edits: Pan Voice Smoothing Pass

## Files changed

- `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex`

## What changed

I made a narrow voice pass over a few sentences that had become slightly too formal or mechanically defensive after the recent source integrations. The edits keep the argument and citations intact while making the prose sound more like the rest of the paper.

Specific changes:

- Recast the opening bracketing paragraph so it states the limit of the argument plainly, while still treating Alter, Pereboom, and Stoljar as serious adjacent options.
- Softened the literature-positioning transition from "adjacent to, but not a substitute for" to a simpler statement about where the discussion belongs.
- Reworked the Section 7 deeper-reply sentence so it says the relevant point directly: sophisticated replies grant that the hidden posits are not first-person given and then defend them as theory.
- Split the Stoljar comparison into a cleaner two-sentence structure, preserving the caveat that the paper does not depend on physicalism or the ignorance hypothesis.
- Changed the conclusion's "paper therefore gives" phrasing to the plainer "result is," without changing the argumentative force.

## Rationale

The goal was not to add new claims. It was to remove a few phrases that sounded too polished, too programmatic, or too much like a literature-review scaffold. The revised sentences are more direct and a little less defensive, while preserving the paper's main qualifications: the argument is a constraint, not a disproof; Russellian and ignorance-based options remain live; and sophisticated panpsychist replies can still be defended on theoretical grounds.
