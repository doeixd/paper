# Pan paper symmetry-with-materialism payoff

## Scope

Added two sentences to the close of Section 5 (The Failure of Warrant Transfer) that make the rhetorical payoff of the warrant-transfer argument explicit. No other changes.

## Why

The paper's warrant-transfer argument is that panpsychism's further posits (micro-experience, proto-experience, cosmic subjectivity, phenomenal bonding, phenomenal-powers grounding) cannot inherit the epistemic privilege of ordinary first-person consciousness. The implication, which the paper carries but does not state directly, is that once panpsychism is defending those posits, it is doing what the functionalist and the materialist were already doing: observing the world, making inferences, and defending the result on the strength of those inferences. That symmetry-with-materialism point is the rhetorical cash-out of the argument; it is what makes the warrant-transfer failure consequential rather than just procedural. The earlier draft said "compete on the same abductive terms as any other metaphysical hypothesis," which carries the same content but at a higher level of abstraction. A reader who already knows the dialectic catches the symmetry; a reader who does not will miss it.

## Change

At the end of Section 5, after "Those further posits may still be defended, but they must earn their warrant as theory rather than inherit it from acquaintance," added:

> "At that point the panpsychist is doing what the functionalist and the materialist were already doing: observing the world, making inferences, and defending the result on the strength of those inferences. The first-person privilege that motivated the position originally no longer applies to the hidden posits it now wants to defend."

This is the rhetorical payoff of the warrant-transfer section, said directly. It is substantive content (the consequence of the argument), not signposting or an announcement of what the paper is about to do.

## What was deliberately not done

- The conclusion was not touched. Its existing "compete on the same abductive terms as any other metaphysical hypothesis" is the abstract version of the same point and closes the paper cleanly. Adding the symmetry sentence both places would be redundant.
- Section 8 (Explanatory Burden) was not touched. It already says, for the specific cases of dreamless sleep and temporal grain, that the posit requires independent warrant and that first-person evidence does not license it. The symmetry framing belongs where the warrant-transfer argument concludes (Section 5), not where the specific illustrations live.
