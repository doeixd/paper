## Style Guide Change

Removed the "No Clause Stacking" rule and its table from `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Replaced with a lighter "prefer clarity over choppiness" principle that allows complex sentences when the complexity serves the idea. The old rule was producing staccato, sentence-stacked prose (itself an AI tell per the anti-AI writing guide). Updated the Phase 2 checklist item to match.

## Anti-AI Prose Pass

Systematic pass targeting the most recognizable AI writing patterns, guided by an anti-AI writing guide focused on structural, vocabulary, and tonal tells.

### 1. "The present paper/view/proposal/criterion/account" (20+ instances reduced to 0)

The single loudest AI tell. Every instance was replaced with "this paper," "this view," "the criterion here," or restructured to avoid the phrase entirely. Human academic writers rarely use "the present paper" more than once or twice.

### 2. "genuinely" (10 instances reduced to 2)

Kept only the two that do real contrastive work (intro: "genuinely autonomous from those that remain parasitic"; Franklin & Robertson's "genuinely novel"). Cut the rest.

### 3. "This is why..." / "This is where..." / "This is also why..." (7 instances reduced to 0)

Formulaic connector pattern. Each replaced with a more varied construction: "because," restructured clauses, "here," or direct statement.

### 4. "straightforward" (1 instance removed)

Red-flag vocabulary. "The philosophical significance is straightforward" became direct statement of the significance.

### 5. Announcer sentences ("The key point is...", "The deeper question is...", "The key principle is...")

Three instances removed. In each case, the prose now begins with the substance rather than announcing it.

### 6. Residual negation structures

Fixed remaining "does not X. It Y" patterns:
- "the paper does not require interventions as metaphysical glue. It requires intervention-invariance" merged
- "This does not reduce formal systems to arbitrary invention" sentence removed (next sentence carries the point)

### 7. Sentence flow and rhythm variance

Added longer, flowing sentences where the prose was staccato:
- Connected short sentence stacks with semicolons, commas, and natural bridges ("because," "and," "yet," "which")
- Varied sentence openers (reduced runs of "That..." paragraph starters)
- Added occasional short punchy sentences ("Fair enough." / "The apparent macro-autonomy disappears.")
- Merged some three-sentence patterns into one flowing sentence to break the uniform-length march

### 8. High-entropy phrases and voice

Small substitutions that add texture without being forced:
- "That is correct as far as it goes" → "Fair enough"
- "utility is available. The present view rejects both." → "utility is available. Both are wrong."
- "Computational mechanics puts the same point in sharper language" (replacing "Equivalent language from computational mechanics clarifies why this is not superficial bookkeeping")

### 9. Varied repeated technical phrases

"under admissible perturbation" (14 occurrences) and "under fixed/declared constraints" (15 occurrences) are borderline technical terms. Varied two instances in prose contexts ("when the system is probed," "holds up under") while keeping them in formal and list contexts where precision matters.
