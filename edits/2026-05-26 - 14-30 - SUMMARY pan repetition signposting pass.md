# Summary of edits: panpsychism mainbody repetition / signposting / cuteness pass

File touched: `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex`

## Motivation

Reviewer-style read of the manuscript flagged three layers of echo / over-structure:

1. Surface formulations recurring too tightly ("not a deliverance of acquaintance," "requires further argument," "theoretical posit").
2. Larger phrasal echoes ("hidden phenomenal X" appearing 5x in Section 7, "does not by itself" formula appearing 6 times across the paper).
3. Structural over-determination: a redundant 5-step enumeration restating the boxed constraint, defensive meta-closers at the ends of sections, an over-enumerated catalogue of phenomenal posits restated ~10 times, and small cuteness moments.

Goal: tighten without dropping qualifications, citations, or argument steps. The paper is shorter and reads cleaner; no claim has been strengthened, weakened, or removed.

## Surface-level variations (echo-reducing)

- Line ~33 (Sec 3, Hidden posits): "Claims about a cosmic subject ... require further argument" → "A cosmic subject behind the pain, or phenomenal natures in the microphysical story of vision, must be argued for on separate grounds." Breaks the parallel with the earlier "Claims about ... require further support."
- Line ~51 (Cosmopsychism): "not a deliverance of first-person acquaintance" → "not something acquaintance presents."
- Line ~55 (Goff hybrid cosmopsychism): "That is theoretical metaphysics, not a direct deliverance of acquaintance." → "That is metaphysical theorizing about how subjects relate to wholes, and introspection does not present it." Adds substance and breaks the third "deliverance" echo.
- Line ~57 (Mørch): "a legitimate theoretical posit" → "a coherent theoretical entity."
- Section 7 "hidden" cluster: five of the six "hidden phenomenal X" / "hidden base" tokens varied to "posited," "undisclosed," or "sub-threshold" as appropriate. The point about hiddenness remains; the lexical chime is broken.
- "Does not by itself" formula: kept the foundational uses (Sec 1 introspection / Sec 4 Goff RM) but varied later instances to "does not, on its own" and "does not thereby."
- "Press"/"presses nearby" appeared 4–5 times across the paper. Varied two (Frankish "bears on this point"; Phenomenal-concept strategies "raise a nearby caution") to thin the cluster.
- "Introspective warrant problem" (coined in passing in the cosmopsychism paragraph, never reused) → "the warrant question." Drops a one-off named concept the paper did not earn.
- "That answer only names the fork." → "That answer relabels the dilemma rather than dissolving it." Debate-team cadence replaced with substantive phrasing.

## Structural cuts

- **Removed the 5-step enumeration** at the old Section 3.1. The boxed-quote statement of the constraint plus the surrounding prose already says everything the enumeration restated; the numbered list functioned as cosmetic logical formalism. Adjusted the "Because the first step concerns..." follow-on to "Because the constraint concerns...".
- **Removed subsection headers 3.1 "The constraint stated" and 3.2 "Hidden posits and first-person warrant."** Section 3 is short and now reads as continuous prose, which suits the argumentative flow better than the over-segmented structure.
- **Cut the Section 4 closer** ("The constraint targets a pattern of warrant, not individual writers..."). Defensive throat-clearing that duplicated the Section 4 opening's disclaimer about uneven application.
- **Cut the Section 7 closer** ("Both illustrations leave the metaphysics open. They show a change in kind of warrant..."). Each of the dreamless-sleep and temporal-grain illustrations now closes itself; the meta-summary added nothing.

## Compressions

- The catalogue "micro-experience, proto-experience, proto-phenomenal categorical bases, cosmic subjectivity, undisclosed phenomenal bases, phenomenal bonding relations, or forms of phenomenal inheritance" appeared in some form ~10 times. Shortened in three places:
  - Section 3 (after constraint statement): "If panpsychism posits such structures..." replacing a long re-enumeration.
  - Section 3 (Hidden posits paragraph): trimmed list from seven items to five.
  - Section 3 (combination paragraph): "warrant for the posits it operates on" replacing a five-item list.
  - Conclusion: "support for such posits" replacing the closing five-item catalogue.
- **Mørch paragraph** (Section 4): trimmed connective tissue ("If successful, this would give panpsychism a substantive explanatory role. It also makes the constraint sharper." and "That concession still leaves a large gap.") and a long agency list ("temporality, intention, goal-representation, bodily feedback, and a distinction between trying and succeeding" → "temporality, intention, embodiment, and the difference between trying and succeeding"). All the argumentative moves remain; the paragraph is now about 25% shorter.
- **Conclusion last paragraph**: removed the tautological middle sentence "Once it moves from acquaintance with phenomenal character to claims about fundamental role, it has moved from acquaintance to theory." The point is already carried by the surrounding sentences.

## What was deliberately left alone

- The opening posit catalogue (Section 1) and the canonical statement of the constraint (Section 3 quote) are kept full; they do scene-setting work the later compressed instances rely on.
- The "two gaps" framework in Section 7 — kept intact since it is the burden-shift argument's core.
- Frankish illusionism qualifier, Saad bracketing, Stoljar ignorance caveat, the Ramm paragraph, and the dreamless-sleep / temporal-grain illustrations — all preserved with their citations and qualifications.
- The Sec 4 application-by-application structure (Strawson → Goff → cosmopsychism → Goff hybrid → Mørch → Roelofs → Ramm) — preserved despite the same-move repetition concern, because dropping any one author would weaken the comparative range the paper deliberately surveys.
- "Acquaintance," "first-person experience," "phenomenal character," "warrant" — high-frequency by design; these are the paper's load-bearing vocabulary.

## Net effect

Mainbody is ~15 lines shorter (153 → ~138) once the enumeration and meta-closers come out. Every argumentative step is intact; what's gone is restatement, formalist scaffolding, defensive meta-statement, and lexical chime. No new claims, no shifted qualifications, no removed citations.
