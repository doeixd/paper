# Summary of edits

I added three new detailed source-summary files in `sources/detailed-source-summaries/`:

- `45-wallace2024.md`
- `46-wimsatt2000.md`
- `47-wimsatt1981.md`

The goal was to match the existing house style used in nearby source-summary files while staying close to the source texts themselves. Each file includes the required sections:

- key heading
- `## Full Citation`
- `## Detailed Summary`
- `## Core Argument Map`
- `## Relation to Real Patterns Need Closure`
- `## Reviewer-Relevant Cautions`
- `## Text Source`

For Wallace, I kept the summary calibrated to the actual structure of the preprint. The summary makes clear that Wallace does not think real patterns alone solve the ontological problem. It foregrounds his mathematics-first structural realism, his notion of predicate precisification, and his claim that ontology is secondary to mathematically presented structure. I also made the preprint status explicit in both the citation and the cautions, since that matters for accurate use.

For Wimsatt 2000, I centered the summary on the four conditions for aggregativity, the decomposition-relative character of the account, and the paper's broader warning against "nothing-but" reductionisms. I was careful not to present Wimsatt as offering a strong anti-reductionist theory of emergence. The file instead preserves his main line, which is that emergence is common, organization-dependent, and compatible with mechanistic reduction.

For Wimsatt 1981, I emphasized robustness as invariance across partially independent routes of access, its role in reliability and realism, its connection to levels and sufficient parameters, and its vulnerability to pseudorobustness when independence fails. The summary keeps the group-selection discussion in its methodological role rather than overstating it as a settled biological verdict.

In the `Relation to Real Patterns Need Closure` sections, I kept the relevance deliberately modest. The summaries point to what each source contributes to RPNC, but they stop short of implying that any of these works already supplies a closure criterion. That restraint matters for reviewer resilience. These sources help motivate, constrain, and sharpen the closure project, but none should be represented as already doing all of that work.

I did not edit `references.md` because the task was limited to source-summary files, and these summaries already contain their own `## Full Citation` sections.
