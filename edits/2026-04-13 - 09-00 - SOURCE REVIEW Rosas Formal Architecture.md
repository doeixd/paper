# Source Review: Rosas Formal Architecture

## Rosas et al.

### Source thesis relevant to this paper
Rosas et al. give a formal account of hierarchical emergence in terms of informational, causal, and computational closure. Their central payoff is a lattice of nested computational structures ordered by coarse-graining, which functions as a blueprint for functional architecture.

### Citation and use suggestions
1. Use this source in `\subsection{Closure and the Primacy of Physics Constraint}` and `\subsection{Why Closure Carries Ontological Weight}` around lines 413-444 and 724-780 to make the paper's transition criterion feel formally anchored. Their software/hardware framing is especially useful for the claim that closure is about autonomy of transition structure, not mere descriptive compression.
2. Use them in `\subsection{Admissibility and Hierarchical Evaluation}` around lines 429-465 and again in `\subsection{Closure, Underdetermination, and Objecthood Discipline}` around lines 880-930 to connect admissible interventions with the objective space of candidate coarse-grainings.
3. Use them in `\subsection{Approximate Closure Without Instrumentalist Drift}` around lines 1200-1303 to justify the graded treatment of closure. Their exact/approximate closure contrast can support the move from robust to qualified to indeterminate without making those verdicts look ad hoc.
4. Use them in `\subsection{Levels of Claim and Representation}` around lines 1650-1671 to keep the paper from collapsing representation into ontology. Their framework distinguishes macroscopic processes, computations, and implementations more cleanly than a simple "useful model" story.
5. Use them in the conclusion around lines 2231-2239 to support the line that the rainforest is a lattice of computationally closed coarse-grainings. That is the cleanest place to turn their formal architecture into the paper's metaphysical payoff.

### What not to claim
- Do not say Rosas et al. themselves supply an ontology of real patterns or a rainforest-admission criterion. They give formal machinery, not the paper's metaphysical interpretation.
- Do not collapse their three closure notions into one. Informational, causal, and computational closure play distinct roles in the source and should stay distinct unless the manuscript explicitly argues for a limited identification.
- Do not treat approximate closure as enough on its own to secure objecthood. The source helps with formal structure, but the paper still needs its own philosophical step from closure to ontological commitment.

### Where the current manuscript underuses or misframes them
- The manuscript already cites them as formal support, but it could use their explicit `lattice` language earlier and more visibly, especially where the paper argues that rainforest realism has a structured space of admissible levels.
- The draft should exploit their software metaphor when explaining why closure is about self-contained macro-transition structure rather than mere compression or classification convenience.
- The current paper should avoid presenting Rosas et al. as if they had already solved the metaphysical question. They provide the formal architecture that makes the philosophical question sharper.
