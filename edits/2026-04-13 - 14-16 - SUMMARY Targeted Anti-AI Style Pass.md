Summary

This pass made a narrow anti-AI style cleanup to the Synthese draft. The goal was not to change the argument or make the paper less academic. It was to reduce a few residual prose habits that can make careful philosophical writing sound slightly over-smoothed or syntactically over-patterned.

Changes made

1. Reduced repetitive contrast framing in the introduction.
   Several sentences built around repeated `rather than` or similarly balanced contrast structures were rewritten into more direct affirmative statements. This keeps the argument clear while making the rhythm feel less mechanically contrastive.

2. Softened over-packaged payoff language in the lattice section.
   A few sentences in the late Section 3 payoff passage were revised so they state the metaphysical point more plainly and with less perfectly mirrored phrasing.

3. Tightened the conclusion's meta-summary voice.
   Formulae such as "What this adds..." and "What the proposal buys..." were revised into more natural academic prose. The goal was to preserve the summary function while reducing the sense of rhetorical packaging.

4. Made one minor tone adjustment in the disclosure section.
   "This disclosure structure is philosophically important" became "This disclosure structure matters philosophically" to make the prose sound a little less templated.

5. Synced the blinded submission source.
   The same local wording changes were copied into the inlined blinded submission file so it remains aligned with the main body source.

Why these edits were made

The paper was already strong in substance and mostly clear of generic AI markers. The remaining issue was subtler: certain passages, especially the introduction, lattice-payoff block, and conclusion, occasionally relied on repeated contrast structures and very polished payoff sentences. In an academic paper, those moves are not inherently bad, but in clusters they can make the prose feel slightly synthetic. This pass keeps the paper's precision, qualifications, and reviewer-facing discipline while giving those passages a more natural argumentative cadence.

What was deliberately not changed

- No claims, citations, examples, or structure were altered.
- No qualifications or scope guards were removed.
- No attempt was made to make the paper informal or blog-like.
- The philosophical distinctions remain intact; only their phrasing was tightened.
