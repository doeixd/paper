I added two connected clarifications.

- In the regime dependence section, I added a sentence explaining that regime-indexing is not a concession to weakness but the mechanism that makes robust verdicts in the special sciences possible at all.
- In the objection about closure being too strong, I added a fuller reply explaining that a macro-variable can be robustly real within a bounded ecological, physiological, or institutional regime even if it leaks outside that regime. Qualified status is triggered when regime boundaries are stretched or when intervention evidence within the regime remains too sparse for a stronger verdict.

Why this matters: it shifts the dialectic from `most special-science kinds only get second-class reality` to `robust standing is available where the regime is genuinely bounded and dynamically stable`. That makes the paper's regime-indexing machinery look necessary rather than ad hoc.
