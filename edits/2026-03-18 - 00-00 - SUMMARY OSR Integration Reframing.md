# 2026-03-18 — OSR Integration: Anchoring the Apex Network in Ontic Structural Realism

## Summary of Changes

This edit reframes the paper's metaphysical commitments by explicitly anchoring the Apex Network in ontic structural realism (OSR) as developed by Ladyman and Ross (2007). Instead of defending a novel metaphysical position (which invites reviewers to treat the paper as speculative metaphysics), we now clearly state that OSR supplies the background metaphysics and that our contribution is epistemological: the mechanism (brittleness-driven pragmatic selection) by which inquiry converges on objective structure.

## Rationale

The old text had an internal contradiction: Section 4.2 claimed the framework is "structural realist," but Section 6.1.1 said it is "neither" ESR nor OSR. The reframing resolves this tension.

More importantly, this is a defensive move. Instead of asking reviewers to evaluate a novel metaphysics alongside a novel epistemology, we outsource the metaphysical burden to a well-established, widely discussed research program. The paper's ambition is narrowed to what a journal article can deliver: not inventing a metaphysics, but providing the missing epistemological engine for an existing one.

The extension to normative domains is acknowledged as genuinely novel, but framed as the manageable claim that the same ontological category (real relational structure) applies more broadly — not as a new metaphysics from scratch.
