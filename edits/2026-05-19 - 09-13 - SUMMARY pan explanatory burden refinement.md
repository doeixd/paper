# Summary of Edits: Pan Explanatory Burden Refinement

Refined the `pan/` paper after reviewing the informal source text for useful material.

## What changed

- Added a restrained explanatory-burden sentence in Section 8: when the hidden phenomenal base does not explain the organized structure of consciousness, it marks where an explanation is wanted more clearly than it supplies one.
- Revised the nearby Section 7 transition to avoid a slogan-like formulation. The passage now says that sophisticated replies can accept the constraint and shift the warrant burden to theoretical explanation.

## Rationale

The informal source text contained a useful point about panpsychist posits functioning as placeholders. The revision imports that point without using the blog's metaphors, empirical specifics, or stronger claims about sleep and temporal thresholds. The result keeps the paper's thesis narrow: panpsychism is not refuted, but its hidden phenomenal ontology must earn theoretical warrant.
