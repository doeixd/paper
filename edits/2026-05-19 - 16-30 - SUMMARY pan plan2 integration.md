## Edits, in manuscript order

### Section 2 (abductive concession)

Inserted three sentences into the abductive-concession paragraph specifying the cost of going abductive. The original passage marked that warrant had changed kind without saying what panpsychism loses. The added sentences identify the loss: panpsychism's first-person motivation funded an apparent pre-emptive dissolution of the explanatory gap, and once the question is allowed to be theoretical, that anti-emergentist relief is no longer paid for in advance. This sets up the matching corollary added to the Conclusion.

### Section 4 (abstraction objection)

Inserted a new paragraph between the dilemma-about-thinning paragraph and the "Minimal subjectivity may be coherent" paragraph. The paragraph addresses the panpsychist reply that the abstraction from human consciousness to a fundamental-level property is no different from abstracting "mass" from heavy bodies. The reply distinguishes three points of disanalogy: mass abstraction moves from determinate to determinable inside a measurement network; phenomenality is not given apart from being some way for someone; and mass is not asked to deliver intrinsic-nature filling on the strength of being known from within. The paragraph concludes that the abstraction may still be made as a theoretical move, but it no longer trades on the special authority of acquaintance. This is the load-bearing reply that prevents the warrant constraint from being dismissed as proves-too-much.

### Section 4 (phenomenology paragraph before Section 4.1)

Inserted a four-sentence paragraph making the direct first-personal point that even the most attenuated waking consciousness is given with some sense of presence or for-me-ness, which is itself phenomenal character. The paragraph explicitly notes that this is a phenomenological point about what acquaintance presents, not a claim about what later report or memory must reconstruct. The placement sets up the reframing of the reportability subsection that follows.

### Section 4.1 (reportability reframe)

Inserted two sentences into the opening of the reportability discussion making explicit that the constraint concerns the use of pure-awareness states as public evidence, not the private episodes themselves. The added sentences forestall the category-mistake reading flagged by the reviewer critique: the paper is not inferring phenomenal structure from the existence of memory traces. The original dilemma structure remains in place; the reframing makes its dialectical role unmistakable.

### Section 5 (panprotopsychism sharpening)

Inserted two sentences into the panprotopsychism passage stating that introspection is, by what it does, acquaintance with experience, and that its reach is whatever reach experience has. So calling a posit proto-experiential in the same breath as ordinary experience does not extend acquaintance to it. The sharpening is consistent with the existing dilemma and avoids any "contradiction" framing.

### Section 6 (macro-to-micro inference caveat)

Inserted two sentences into the structure-and-dynamics discussion noting that inferring the intrinsic nature of the fundamental from a salient feature of the macro-level requires more than the feature's salience to us. The move needs an independent reason to think that the right filler for an underdetermined categorical role is just the one item we happen to know from within. The caveat does not commit the paper either way on Ontic Structural Realism.

### Section 7 (smoking-gun quotes)

Lifted the existing "fundamental given natural fact" Strawson quote into the body of Section 7 with a charitable preface noting that the phrasing is not a slip and marks exactly the move the paper questions. Added a parallel treatment of Goff's Revelation thesis in the Goff passage, framing it as the move the paper questions while preserving the framing that acquaintance may reveal something about phenomenal character without revealing the metaphysical role of the experience. Both citations use page references already present in the existing manuscript and bibliography.

### Section 7 (cosmopsychism, boundedness and valence)

Inserted three sentences into the cosmopsychism passage noting that ordinary perspectival consciousness is bounded and affectively grained, that perspectival closure and affective valence both belong to what the datum discloses, and that cosmic subjectivity lacks the perspectival closure that ordinary acquaintance reliably exhibits. The boundedness point complements the existing Shani citation by adding a feature of the datum cosmopsychism does not naturally match, without claiming a "flat contradiction" between cosmopsychism and the disclosed datum. Valence is folded into this passage rather than added separately, since it functions as one more feature of what acquaintance discloses.

### Conclusion (relocated-hard-problem corollary)

Inserted a five-sentence paragraph as the new third paragraph of the Conclusion. The paragraph states the corollary that the paper's argument supports: panpsychism's first-person motivation appeared to dissolve the explanatory gap by placing phenomenality at the fundamental level, but once first-person inheritance is removed and the hidden phenomenal level is admitted as theoretical, that pre-emptive dissolution is no longer free. The gap reappears at the seam between the hidden base and the consciousness we actually undergo. Goff's recent treatment of subjects as strongly emergent while phenomenal properties are weakly emergent is cited as making that seam visible within his own framework. The paragraph closes with the measured formulation that panpsychism is entitled to try to close the gap but is not entitled to claim the first-person datum has already closed it.
