## Final Compression and Hardening Pass

### What changed

This pass focused on compression, concentration, and rhetorical discipline rather than further expansion.

In the introduction, I cut a large amount of literature-positioning and repeated framing while preserving the paper's core novelty claim, Franklin-Robertson setup, causation clarification, and scope caveats. The goal was to get the reader to the criterion more quickly and reduce the sense that the paper is repeatedly restating the same thesis in different vocabularies.

In the Section 3 common-cause discussion, I added one compact argument-map paragraph that states the wedge against screening off in a single concentrated sequence: what Franklin and Robertson require, why the proxy can satisfy that package observationally, what further question closure asks, and why the proxy fails there. After adding that paragraph, I trimmed nearby repetition in both the Section 3 discussion and the `Beyond Screening Off` objection reply so that the wedge reads as one decisive move rather than a distributed one.

In Section 2, I recast the composition discussion in a narrower register. The text no longer reads as though the paper is offering a general answer to the Special Composition Question. It now presents composition claims as a limited consequence within the paper's own macro-objecthood register.

I also reduced Section 2.12 substantially. The Pearl discussion is now compact, the mechanistic comparison is brief, and the Newman point is folded into a shorter anti-triviality contrast. The section no longer reads like a mini-survey.

Across Sections 2 through 6, I softened a few phrases that risked overstating the metaphysical payoff. The lattice claim remains, but the strongest formulations now speak in terms of what robust higher-level realism amounts to on the present view, or what tracks ontological seriousness on the present account, rather than sounding like the paper has settled higher-level ontology without remainder.

Finally, I cleaned up a few reviewer-facing style points. `macro-what-follows` is now defined at first operational use, and the verdict paragraph now speaks of supporting realist commitment rather than licensing ontology talk in a way that made the paper sound too manual-like.

### Why these changes were made

The recent reviews were right that the paper's remaining problems were no longer primarily missing arguments. They were concentration, pacing, and overstatement problems. The manuscript had the right materials, but too many of them were spread across multiple sections or repeated in slightly different language.

This pass was designed to make the central claim land faster: closure is the further condition for robust macro-objecthood under the paper's stated commitments. The paper now spends less time re-announcing that claim, states the common-cause wedge more decisively, and avoids inviting collateral debates, especially on general composition.

### Objections this pass is meant to pre-empt

- A reviewer saying the common-cause wedge is still too spread out to see its exact logical structure.
- A reviewer feeling that Sections 1 and 2 repeat the same thesis too many times before the argument fully moves.
- A reviewer reading the lattice passages as claiming more metaphysical finality than the paper actually earns.
- A reviewer treating the composition sentence as an unnecessary invitation to a broader mereology dispute.
- A reviewer reading the verdict discussion as a methodological manual rather than as a clarification of ontology-reporting consequences.

### Remaining pressure points

The common-cause proxy remains the paper's most likely point of pressure, even after concentration. The argument is now easier to locate and harder to deflect, but that remains the section a skeptical referee is most likely to probe.

The admissibility framework still carries substantial philosophical weight, especially outside cleaner physical examples. The paper now handles that issue more explicitly, but future passes should continue checking that the prose remains candid about where the hard work sits.

The lattice payoff is now better proportioned to the argument, but it still depends on the paper's own philosophical move from formal closure diagnostics to disciplined ontology-reporting. That is appropriate, though it remains the most likely site of principled disagreement from a skeptical reader.
