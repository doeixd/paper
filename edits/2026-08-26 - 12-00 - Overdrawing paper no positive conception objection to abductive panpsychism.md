# Overdrawing on Experience: adding the "no positive conception" objection

File: `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex` (+~690 words). SN wrapper rebuilt (17 pp).

## Rationale
The paper already argued that careful panpsychists who retreat to abduction forfeit first-person authority. A reviewer could still sidestep the whole paper by saying "fine, the view is theoretical, not phenomenological." The new material closes that exit: the abductive case itself is weakened, because bare subjectivity and proto-experience have no positive content beyond their theoretical role. Our only working conception of the experiential is structured experience.

## Changes
1. **Section 2 (abductive reply paragraph):** preview of the new question, so the baton passes forward.
2. **Section 4 (after the "thinning the posit" paragraph):** new paragraph. Two ways to specify the thin posit (subtraction, role); neither gives an independent positive conception. Pre-empts the "analogy with our own case" reply: any carried-over feature reintroduces structure (first horn of the existing dilemma); otherwise the analogy is verbal.
3. **Section 5 (structure/dynamics):** direct engagement with Goff's acquaintance advantage (goff2017cfr, 106-132). Concede the advantage for structured experience; deny transfer to proto/bare fillers, where the panpsychist is in the same position as the physicalist. The motivating asymmetry disappears where the filling is done.
4. **Section 7 (explanatory burden):** new paragraph tying the point to IBE. Brute emergence, unknown non-phenomenal categorical nature, and proto-phenomenal nature occupy the same explanatory slot; the panprotopsychist dilemma is a special case. Handles the "quarks are known only by role" objection. Credits Morch's phenomenal powers as the right kind of response (theory doing the work). Added a sentence to the Saad/Mendelovici paragraph scoping the objection to the bare/proto posit, not every abductive argument.
5. **Conclusion:** one sentence: forfeiture is doubled for the thinnest posits.

No new named concepts, no new references, no em dashes, no footnotes.
