## Reviewer Response Consolidation Pass

### What changed

This pass concentrated on the last live reviewer-vulnerability points without reopening the paper's structure.

In the introduction, I removed the standalone `Three Nearby Positions` and `Contribution Map` subsections and folded their content into one compressed positioning-and-roadmap paragraph. The aim was to get the reader to the criterion faster while preserving the Franklin-Robertson setup, the closure thesis, the projectibility preview, and the paper's existing non-overclaim safeguards.

In the common-cause discussion, I strengthened the anti-deflection wording in both the late Section 3 reconstruction and the `Beyond Screening Off` reply. The paper now says more directly that the relevant screening off is observationally genuine relative to the target explanandum, even though it remains insufficient for robust macro-objecthood. This keeps the wedge cumulative rather than hostile while making it harder to answer the case by simply saying the screening off was never real.

In the Rosas-to-ontology bridge, I made the lattice claim thesis-level once, in one controlled location. The paper now states that, on the present view, the rainforest is the lattice of admissible computationally closed coarse-grainings, and that admission to that lattice is what robust higher-level realism amounts to under the paper's stated commitments. The surrounding prose still preserves the paper's qualifications about admissibility, regime, and interpretation.

After the prose revisions, I synchronized the blinded submission source with the mainbody file and rebuilt both the Springer wrapper and the blinded submission PDF.

### Why these changes were made

The review was right that the paper's remaining vulnerabilities were no longer mainly about ambition, but about deflectability and pacing. The common-cause wedge needed a cleaner answer to the thought that Franklin-Robertson could absorb the case as merely misdiagnosed screening off. The lattice claim needed to be stated once as a thesis rather than remaining only a distributed payoff. The introduction still asked the reader to absorb too much front-loaded mapping before arriving at the criterion itself.

These edits were designed to fix those points with minimal architectural disturbance. The paper is now more direct about where it disagrees with nearby views, more explicit about its central metaphysical claim, and lighter at the front end.

### Objections this pass is meant to pre-empt

- A reviewer saying the common-cause proxy never genuinely satisfied screening off in the first place.
- A reviewer missing the paper's central lattice claim because it appeared only as a recurring payoff rather than a thesis-level statement.
- A reviewer feeling over-managed by the introduction before reaching the actual criterion.
- A reviewer reading the manuscript as stronger in aspiration than in explicitly stated payoff.

### Remaining pressure points

The Franklin-Robertson wedge is now harder to deflect, but it remains the paper's most delicate dialectical area. Future passes should still check that the prose reads as constructive refinement rather than adversarial correction.

The lattice claim is now stated clearly once, but the philosophical step from formal closure structure to ontology-reporting verdicts remains the place where the paper asks the reader to follow its own argument rather than a source's ready-made conclusion. That is appropriate, but it will remain a likely pressure point for a skeptical reviewer.

The sparse-intervention material, admissibility discussion, and verdict consequences are now more explicit than before. Still, those sections should continue to be watched for drift toward either overconfidence or unnecessary procedural detail in future revisions.
