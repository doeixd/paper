# Summary of edits: panpsychism mainbody — exhibiting the abductive/privilege tension

File touched: `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-mainbody.tex` (Section 4 opener)

## Motivation

The Section 4 opener (in the current numbering, the first paragraph of "Applications to Panpsychist Views") already framed the central pattern the paper is targeting: panpsychists who "argue abductively in their official statements while still drawing dialectical force from first-person privilege." Until now this was an assertion, not a demonstration. Several panpsychists make the tension visible in their own words — the *official* argument is presented as theoretical (simplicity, theoretical posits, abductive considerations), but the *dialectical work* leans on the special epistemic status of consciousness itself. Exhibiting this contrast with direct quotation strengthens the framing claim and gives the case-by-case applications that follow a clearer pattern to substantiate.

## What was added

After the existing framing sentence ("It also bears on views that argue abductively in their official statements while still drawing dialectical force from first-person privilege"), a three-quote demonstration was inserted:

- Goff's official framing as resting on "simplicity grounds" (citing `goff2017cfr`).
- M{\o}rch's framing of phenomenal properties as "posited as the realizers of all physical structure" (citing `morch2018`).
- Roelofs treating one's own consciousness as "a data-point in favour of panpsychism" (citing `roelofs2019`) — the privilege side of the same dialectic.

The passage ends with the Saad bracketing already in the opener, which now functions more naturally as a contrast to the just-shown pattern.

## Editorial choices

- **Dropped a planned Strawson reuse.** The "fundamental given natural fact" quote appears in the Strawson application paragraph already; reusing it in the cluster would re-introduce a near-term lexical echo. The Roelofs quote does the same dialectical work in fewer words.
- **Avoided "deliverances of introspection."** Earlier passes varied three instances of "deliverance of acquaintance" because they were echoing. Re-seeding "deliverances" here — even in a description of the panpsychists' framing — would undo that work. The neutral "direct introspective claims" is used instead, but in fact the final draft uses "their official statements" / "the official framing is theoretical" wording that avoids the term entirely.
- **Three quotes, not four or five.** A denser cluster would distract from the rest of the opener. Three is enough to show both sides of the dialectic (two abductive, one privilege).
- **Placement: Section 4 opener, before Saad.** Earlier candidate placement was end of Section 3 (after "carry more weight than the abduction earns"). Section 4 opener was chosen because it is where the paper explicitly frames the pattern, so the demonstration lands on the relevant claim. End of Section 3 would have substantiated the same point but at a moment where the reader has just been shown the formal constraint; the quotes are more useful right when the application phase begins.
