## What the review asked for, and what we did

### Accepted

**Novelty candor (Major #1).** Added a paragraph at the end of Section 2 explicitly stating which moves are inherited (Lin on selfless states, Windt on dreamless sleep, Frankish on depsychologization, Chalmers/Coleman on combination, the standard structure/dynamics rejoinder) and which are the paper's distinctive contribution (the synthesizing constraint, the forfeited-dialectical-advantage point, and the fittingness observation). Honest without being self-deprecating.

**Bare-subjectivity dilemma sharpening (Major #4).** Added a paragraph in Section 4 addressing the primitivity reply. A minimal phenomenal character can be primitive at the fundamental level (not further analyzable) while still being determinate. The reply concedes the dilemma's first horn but denies its consequence. Two responses: any determinate primitive still has the question why it has the form it has, even when no further phenomenal analysis is available; and the route from a primitive micro-character to determinate macro-consciousness remains owed.

**Revelation engagement (Major #3).** Added a focused paragraph in Section 5 directly engaging revelation. Goff's strong version delivers the essence; Roelofs's moderate version delivers less. The introspective warrant constraint runs even on the moderate version: revelation discloses what is given in acquaintance, and what is given is given at the grain of ordinary experience.

**Corollary preview (Minor #11).** Added a two-sentence preview at the end of Section 1, with the Goff 2024 anchor.

**Stoljar engagement (Minor #9).** Added two sentences in Section 8 explicitly stating that the constraint is compatible with the ignorance view but does not require it.

**Fittingness expansion (Minor #6).** Added three sentences in the Section 2 abductive paragraph identifying the two halves of the fittingness assumption (categorical role exists; experience is the right kind of item to fill it) and noting that each half is theory rather than datum.

**Roelofs methodological-vs-rhetorical tightening (Major #2).** Added a short paragraph in Section 7 distinguishing what the constraint constrains. When individual panpsychists are methodologically clean, the constraint registers that practice rather than restricting it. What the constraint targets is the position as it circulates in the wider literature.

**Reportability slide fix (Minor #5).** Reworded the Windt-related passage so it cleanly attributes claims to what the report can establish rather than to the state itself.

**Sleep / temporal-grain hedging cut (Minor #8).** Dropped "provided it is handled carefully," "modest point," and "conceptual illustrations rather than empirical proofs against panpsychism" hedges. Let the examples do their work.

**Transferable-credit mannerism cut (Style).** Replaced the motto-like "First-person warrant is not a transferable credit" with direct philosophical prose: "The warrant attaches to what is given, not to whatever a theory chooses to describe in similar terms."

### Declined

**Major #2 (constraint doesn't bite abductive panpsychists).** Declined the reviewer's implicit suggestion that the paper's target is vanishing. The Strawson and Goff quotes added in a previous pass already show the rhetorical move happening in foundational texts. The new Section 7 paragraph clarifies that the constraint is aimed at the position-as-it-circulates, not at individual methodological hygiene. That answers the worry without overhauling.

**Minor #6 alternative: fittingness as its own section.** Declined. The fittingness expansion stays in place (Section 2). A separate section would dilute the diagnostic by splitting it from the abductive-concession argument where it belongs.

**Minor #10 (cut 1000–1500 words).** Declined. The paper at ~9,500 words sits within the normal range for philosophy of mind articles. The repetition the reviewer flags in Sections 5 and 7 is the dialectically load-bearing kind, not bloat. The hedging cuts and mannerism cleanup serve the spirit of the suggestion without surgery.
