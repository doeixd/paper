# Summary of edits

Made a small prose-focused revision pass on the panpsychism paper after checking for signposting, announcing, repeated caveat forms, negation clusters, and stock AI-like phrasing.

Changes made:

- Recast the introduction's quality/role distinction so it reads as an argument rather than an announcement.
- Reduced several "not by itself" and "does not" constructions where the same point could be stated positively.
- Replaced some signposting phrases with direct claims, especially around transparency, revelation, combination, cosmopsychism, and the burden shift.
- Smoothed the applications section so it no longer relies on repeated "Views that..." sentence stacking.
- Kept the load-bearing qualifications for first-person-motivated panpsychism, purely theoretical panpsychist arguments, cosmopsychic individuation, Mørch's phenomenal powers view, and primitive phenomenal character.
- Replaced the slightly rhetorical "stripped-down causal power" phrasing with "minimal causal power."
- Replaced a leftover "top-down structure" phrase with plainer "whole-to-part structure."
- Preserved the central caveats: the paper does not reject panpsychism as such, does not deny pure-awareness or minimal-consciousness reports, does not solve the hard problem, and does not claim that determinate phenomenal character must be reductively explained.

Build:

- Rebuilt the Springer PDF with `pdflatex -> bibtex -> pdflatex -> pdflatex`.
- Copied the compiled PDF to `pan/overdrawing-on-experience-sn.pdf`.
- Checked the LaTeX log for undefined citations and unresolved references. None were found.

Rationale:

The goal was to make the paper sound more like careful academic prose and less like an AI-generated argument map. The edits preserve the paper's defenses while reducing overly explicit scaffolding, repetitive negation, and phrases that sounded too prosecutorial or too neat.
