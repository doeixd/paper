# Summary of Edits: Restored Contribution and Mass Analogy

## File Edited

## Purpose

This pass checked whether the anti-AI polish had removed any load-bearing philosophical content. Two small losses were identified and restored in compact form.

## Changes

1. **Restored the contribution claim.** The literature-positioning paragraph now states more explicitly that the paper adds an account of what panpsychism forfeits when the first-person route is treated as abduction, and that it exposes the further assumption that experience is the right kind of item to fill the categorical role left open by structural physics.

2. **Restored the deeper mass-analogy point.** The abstraction reply now keeps the smoother version while adding that mass has an identity fixed by its role in theory and practice, not by felt heaviness. This preserves the contrast with phenomenality, whose identification is harder to separate from givenness in experience.

## Rationale

The aim was to avoid a common revision failure: making the prose smoother while silently weakening the argument. These additions recover the substantive pressure points without returning to the longer, more mechanical wording from earlier drafts.
