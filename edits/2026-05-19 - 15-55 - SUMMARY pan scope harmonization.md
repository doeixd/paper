# Pan paper scope-harmonization pass

## Scope

Two small wording changes to bring the unqualified uses of "panpsychism" in the introduction and the conclusion's final paragraph into harmony with the Saad scope carve-out added in the previous pass.

## Why

The earlier integration pass made Section 8 explicitly carve Saad-style harmony arguments out of the present argument's reach. After that carve-out, two sentences elsewhere in the paper still spoke of "panpsychism" overdrawing in unqualified terms, which read slightly more sweepingly than the now-careful scope warrants. Both sentences were technically compatible with the carve-out (since the surrounding context implies the first-person-motivated cases), but the unqualified subject was a small dissonance worth removing.

## Changes

1. **Introduction, opening of the thesis paragraph.** "Panpsychism often overdraws on the evidential authority of first-person experience" became "In its first-person-motivated forms, panpsychism often overdraws on the evidential authority of first-person experience." The fronted qualifier makes the scope explicit at the first place the thesis is stated.

2. **Conclusion's final paragraph.** "Panpsychism overdraws on experience when it treats..." was reordered as "Where panpsychism treats the epistemic privilege of ordinary consciousness as support for a hidden phenomenal ontology, it overdraws on experience." The same content with the scope condition fronted. The follow-up sentence "Panpsychism is the central case considered here" became "Panpsychism in its first-person-motivated forms is the central case considered here," matching the introduction's qualifier.

## What was deliberately not done

- **The reviewer's separate suggestion to develop the "what does the hidden base actually explain?" challenge in Section 8 by pressing Mørch's phenomenal powers specifically.** The reviewer flagged this as "a request for an additional contribution, not a fix for a flaw." Expanding Section 8 to press individual panpsychist proposals would shift the paper's center of gravity away from the warrant argument, which earlier passes deliberately preserved. The current treatment of dreamless sleep and temporal grain as conceptual illustrations remains appropriate for the paper's scope.
